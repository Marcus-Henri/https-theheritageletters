Nominate a Heritage Voice

Tell us who they are and why. That is all we need to begin.

What happens next. Every nomination is read personally. If the story is one we can tell properly, we will write back and arrange a conversation — usually an hour, sometimes more. At that stage we will ask for the fuller details: where and when they were born, the work they did, the moments they are proudest of, what they would do differently, and a photograph from their youth alongside one from today. None of that is needed now.
